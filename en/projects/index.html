Contributions, results & full validation scope

  • Implemented PID tracking on simplified joint dynamics and analyzed end-effector error, peak error and control torque.
  • Built URDF and PyBullet visualization and 3-DOF gravity-compensated torque control, checking geometric coordinate alignment.

Recorded results

  • Archived MATLAB plots, CSV files and analysis for four trajectory methods form a complete planning-to-control simulation workflow.
  • Archive checks passed planar and spatial numerical tests in MATLAB R2022a and two Python source-wiring checks.
  • The documentation separates 2-DOF kinematic playback from 3-DOF gravity-enabled dynamics control.

Validation scope & limitations

  • I independently developed the modeling and control simulation; other members' reporting and presentation work is not claimed as mine.
  • All control results are simulated and concern end-effector position. There are no physical-arm, gripper or object-contact experiments.
  • MATLAB uses simplified independent-joint dynamics. PyBullet uses different dynamics, parameters and gains, so their metrics are not interchangeable.
  • Archiving did not repeat full PyBullet gravity simulation; Python source-wiring checks are not simulation acceptance.
