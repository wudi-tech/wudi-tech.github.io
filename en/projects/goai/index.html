00 / Context & objective

Multicamera vision-language-action policy adaptation, training deployment, local closed-loop simulation and failure analysis.

01My contributions

  • Adapted head and wrist camera inputs, language instructions, and 14-dimensional robot state and action interfaces.
  • Configured action-head fine-tuning and later multitask training, managing checkpoints, normalization settings and frozen versions.
  • Connected a WebSocket policy service to multiple simulation environments and organized recovery checks and evaluation guardrails.
  • Organized two full evaluations, candidate-checkpoint screening and task-level failure analysis with traceable results and video indices.

02System flow

  1. 01Three cameras and robot state
  2. 02Vision-language-action policy
  3. 03WebSocket policy service
  4. 0414D actions and simulation
  5. 05Task-level evaluation and failure analysis

03Recorded results

  • The stable baseline achieved 33/600 and the 20k fine-tune 28/600 across 24 tasks each: 1,200 episodes in total.
  • Candidate C checkpoints at 60k, 80k and 100k received 45 screening episodes in total, each scoring 0/15; no full 600-episode reevaluation was completed.
  • Diagnostic material covers 29 representative episodes and an index of 87 three-camera videos.

04Validation scope & limitations

Read results alongside their measurement targets and conditions. Historical records, host checks, and device validation are distinguished.

  • StarVLA, QwenOFT, XPolicyLab and RoboDojo provide the upstream foundations. My work is adaptation, configuration, integration, runtime fixes and analysis.
  • These are local simulation records without a confirmed official score. The 20k aggregate observation is below baseline and does not establish improvement.
  • The 45-episode screening is not directly rankable against full 600-episode evaluations; no performance advantage is established for the 100k candidate.
  • Archiving did not rerun GPU training or full simulation. Raw 20k episodes, complete ACT source and implementations for some historical tests remain incomplete.
